Our vision

Every day we try to exceed what our customers expect, and to earn their trust and satisfaction. With a strict focus on quality, we aim to grow further in the worldwide marble business and take on greater projects without compromising on perfection.

Our company

The history of Kouklas Marble spans more than two decades, and with it an international reputation.

We deliver slabs, standard-sized tiles and cut-to-size pieces. Long-term partnerships with quarries give us access to a large stock.
